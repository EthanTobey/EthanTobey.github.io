CYM-KO

Dive into the vibrant world of CYM-KO, where color is power—swap hues, battle ink-hungry foes, and reclaim stolen cartridges to restore your world’s brilliance!

Gameplay

CYM-KO is a fast-paced action-platformer that challenges players to retrieve stolen ink cartridges and restore color to their world. With a unique color-switching mechanic, players must adapt their attacks, defenses, and movement to overcome a variety of enemies and environmental obstacles. The game features three distinct platforming levels filled with hazards and foes, culminating in intense boss battles where strategic color usage is key to victory. As players progress, they unlock powerful abilities tied to the CMYK color model, making for dynamic combat and engaging platforming challenges.

Technologies and Contributions

CYM-KO was developed using Unity and C# scripting, with all art and sound assets created in-house by the development team. My role on the project included gameplay programming and GitHub project management, ensuring smooth collaboration among team members. I was responsible for implementing key gameplay mechanics, such as movement systems, enemy and boss attack patterns, and combat interactions. Through careful iteration and playtesting, I helped refine the game’s mechanics to deliver a polished and engaging player experience.
